# Clarity Audit (Quick Guide)

## Purpose
Identify the few priorities that really matter and remove distractions.

## Inputs
- Current goals (quarter/month/week)
- Active projects & owners
- Known blockers

## Steps (30–45 min)
1) Outcome: What must be true in the next 2 weeks?
2) Focus: What 3 priorities move us toward that outcome?
3) Reality: What’s blocking progress now?
4) Plan: Assign owners + dates for the next 7–14 days.

## Output
- 3 priorities with owners/dates
- Risks + mitigation
- Next review date

## Tip
Clarity before speed. If anything doesn’t support the outcome, pause it.
