# Meeting Rhythm (Lightweight)

## Cadence
- Mon 20m: Priorities & owners (no status)
- Wed 15m: Risks & unblock
- Fri 15m: Review & adjustments

## Rules
- One doc of record (Notion/Doc)
- Decisions captured in-line
- Parking lot for non-essentials

## Monday Agenda (20m)
1) Re-state outcomes (2m)
2) Confirm top 3–5 priorities (5m)
3) Owners & dates (8m)
4) Risks to watch (5m)

## Friday Review (15m)
- What moved the needle?
- What didn’t (and why)?
- What we stop or pause next week
